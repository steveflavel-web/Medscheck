How it works

Step 1

Pharmacy provides medication-history information using an agreed secure process.

Step 2

A pharmacist-reviewed consultation draft is prepared.

Step 3

The in-store pharmacist uses the printable report with the patient and finalises clinical decisions.

Step 4

The pharmacy manages its own eligibility, consultation, claiming and clinical responsibility.

This tool is a planning calculator only, not a clinical decision-making or program claiming system.
